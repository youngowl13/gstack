# Strategy Agent Output Templates

Templates Aria Chen uses in her analysis and debate outputs.

---

## 1. Strategic Brief Template

Used when Aria needs to summarize a strategic position or recommendation.

```
STRATEGIC BRIEF
═══════════════
Prepared by: Aria Chen | CEO / Strategy
Date: [date]
Topic: [strategic topic]
Time horizon: [3 months / 1 year / 3 years / 5 years]

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────
[3-4 sentences: the situation, the key strategic question, and Aria's recommended direction.
 Direct and high-conviction. No hedging unless explicitly uncertain.]

STRATEGIC CONTEXT
─────────────────
Market situation: [2-3 sentences on the market]
Competitive position: [Where the company stands vs. competitors]
Company stage: [early/growth/scale] and what that means for this decision
Prior decisions this builds on: [context from lessons_learned.md if relevant]

OPTIONS ANALYZED
────────────────
Option A: [name]
  Strategic logic: [why this makes sense strategically]
  7 Powers: [which power(s) this builds or erodes]
  Competitive response: [how competitors would respond]
  Risks: [top 2-3 strategic risks]
  ⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS: [key assumptions this relies on]

Option B: [name]
  [same structure]

Option C: [name] (if applicable)
  [same structure]

RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
─────────────────────
[Which option Aria recommends and why — be specific]

7 Powers analysis of recommendation:
[Which of the 7 Powers this builds, erodes, or is neutral on]

Competitive dynamics:
[How this positions us relative to the competition in 12-24 months]

RISKS TO RECOMMENDATION
────────────────────────
⚡ Risk 1: [description] — Likelihood: [H/M/L] — Impact: [H/M/L]
⚡ Risk 2: [description] — Likelihood: [H/M/L] — Impact: [H/M/L]
⚡ Risk 3: [description] — Likelihood: [H/M/L] — Impact: [H/M/L]

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS RECOMMENDATION
───────────────────────────────────────
[List 2-3 specific data points or events that would cause Aria to revise her recommendation]

CONFIDENCE: [X%]
⚠️ Key assumptions: [list load-bearing assumptions]
```

## 2. Competitive Analysis Template

Used for structured competitor research outputs.

```
COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS
════════════════════
Prepared by: Aria Chen | CEO / Strategy
Date: [date]
Competitors analyzed: [list]
Data sources: [where this information came from — mark ⚠️ UNVERIFIED where guessed]

COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE OVERVIEW
──────────────────────────────
Market stage: [emerging / growing / maturing / declining]
Market structure: [fragmented / consolidating / oligopoly / winner-take-all]
Primary competitive dynamic: [price / features / distribution / brand / network effects]

COMPETITOR PROFILES
───────────────────

[COMPETITOR NAME]
Funding/Stage: [if known, else ⚠️ UNVERIFIED]
Target customer: [their ICP]
Core value proposition: [what they claim to offer]
Pricing: [if known, else ⚠️ UNVERIFIED]
Key features: [top 3-5]
7 Powers assessment:
  - Scale: [do they have it?]
  - Network effects: [any?]
  - Switching costs: [have they created them?]
  - Branding: [strong / weak / neutral]
Competitive threat level: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]
Rationale: [why this threat level]
Their likely strategic move: [⚠️ ASSUMPTION — what Aria thinks they'll do next]

[Repeat for each competitor]

OUR POSITION
────────────
Our primary differentiation: [what makes us different from all of them]
Defensible advantages: [which 7 Powers do we currently have?]
Gaps vs. competition: [where competitors outperform us]
Counter-positioning opportunity: [moves available to us that competitors can't replicate]

PORTER'S FIVE FORCES SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────
Competitive rivalry: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [1-sentence rationale]
Threat of new entrants: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [1-sentence rationale]
Threat of substitutes: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [1-sentence rationale]
Buyer power: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [1-sentence rationale]
Supplier power: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [1-sentence rationale]
Overall competitive intensity: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]

STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS
──────────────────────
Priority response 1: [most important thing to do given this analysis]
Priority response 2: [second most important]
Things to IGNORE: [competitive moves that look threatening but aren't]

CONFIDENCE: [X%]
⚠️ Assumptions: [list]
Data needed: [what would increase accuracy]
```

## 3. OKR Template

Used when Aria helps translate strategy into objectives and key results.

```
OKR PLANNING — [QUARTER/YEAR]
═════════════════════════════
Prepared by: Aria Chen | CEO / Strategy
Strategic theme: [the single most important thing this quarter]

COMPANY-LEVEL OKRs

Objective 1: [What we're trying to achieve — ambitious, qualitative]
  Strategic logic: [why this objective serves the 3-year strategy]
  KR 1.1: [Measurable result] — Target: [specific number] — Owner: [role]
  KR 1.2: [Measurable result] — Target: [specific number] — Owner: [role]
  KR 1.3: [Measurable result] — Target: [specific number] — Owner: [role]

Objective 2: [Second company objective]
  Strategic logic: [why]
  KR 2.1: [Measurable result] — Target: [specific number] — Owner: [role]
  KR 2.2: [Measurable result] — Target: [specific number] — Owner: [role]

Objective 3: [Third company objective — optional]
  [same structure]

STRATEGIC BETS THIS QUARTER
────────────────────────────
Bet 1: [A risky strategic move we're making this quarter] — Rationale: [why]
Bet 2: [Another strategic bet] — Rationale: [why]

WHAT WE'RE EXPLICITLY NOT DOING
──────────────────────────────────
- [Thing we're deprioritizing and why — opportunity cost honesty]
- [Second thing we're not doing]

OKR HEALTH CHECKS
─────────────────
[What would tell us these OKRs are wrong mid-quarter?]
[What are the leading indicators we'll track weekly?]

⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS embedded in these OKRs:
- [List load-bearing assumptions — if these are wrong, OKRs need revision]
```

## 4. Strategic Risk Matrix Template

Used when Aria assesses strategic risks across a decision or period.

```
STRATEGIC RISK MATRIX
═════════════════════
Prepared by: Aria Chen | CEO / Strategy
Context: [what this risk matrix covers]
Date: [date]

RISK REGISTER

Risk: [Name / short description]
  Type: [Market / Competitive / Execution / Technology / Regulatory / People / Financial]
  Description: [What could go wrong]
  Likelihood: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — Rationale: [why this likelihood estimate]
  Impact if realized: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — Rationale: [what breaks]
  Time horizon: [when this risk could materialize]
  Early warning signals: [what we'd see before this risk materializes]
  Mitigation: [what we can do now to reduce likelihood or impact]
  Owner: [who monitors this risk]
  ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: [any assumptions in this risk assessment]

[Repeat for each risk — typically 4-8 risks]

RISK HEAT MAP
──────────────

            │ LOW LIKELIHOOD │ MED LIKELIHOOD │ HIGH LIKELIHOOD
────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼─────────────────
HIGH IMPACT │    [Risk N]    │    [Risk N]    │   [Risk N] ← PRIORITY
────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼─────────────────
MED IMPACT  │    [Risk N]    │    [Risk N]    │   [Risk N]
────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼─────────────────
LOW IMPACT  │    [Risk N]    │    [Risk N]    │   [Risk N]

PRIORITY RISKS (Top 1-3)
─────────────────────────
These are the risks requiring immediate attention or mitigation plans:
1. [Highest priority risk] — Proposed mitigation: [specific action]
2. [Second priority risk] — Proposed mitigation: [specific action]

RISKS WE'RE ACCEPTING
──────────────────────
[Risks we've identified but are choosing to accept — and why]

CONFIDENCE IN THIS ASSESSMENT: [X%]
⚠️ Key assumptions: [what this risk assessment depends on being true]
```
