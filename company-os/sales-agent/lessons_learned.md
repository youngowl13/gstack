## Example Entry 1
## [2025-02-10] — sales-outreach
**Situation**: Lost a mid-market deal ($18K ARR) to a competitor despite 3 demo calls and 30 days in the pipeline. Post-loss interview revealed the prospect had been evaluating the competitor simultaneously from day 1.
**What we got wrong**: No competitive intelligence gathered during qualification. We assumed we were the primary vendor. Budget and timeline questions were answered but we never asked "who else are you evaluating?"
**Root cause**: MEDDIC checklist wasn't being applied consistently. "Competition" field was empty in the CRM for this deal.
**Sales process change**: Added a mandatory "Competition" checkpoint to every MEDDIC review. Any deal without a known competitive landscape after demo call 1 is flagged as incomplete qualification. Jordan now asks explicitly: "Who else is on your shortlist?" in every discovery call.

## Example Entry 2
## [2025-03-18] — founder-decision
**Situation**: Debated whether to offer a 40% discount to close a large deal ($60K ARR) at end of quarter. Offered the discount. Closed the deal. Six months later, the account expects the same discount at renewal and is using it as a negotiation anchor.
**What we got wrong**: Discounting without a documented business reason creates a precedent. The discount was not logged with a justification, so CS had no context during renewal and was blindsided.
**Root cause**: Discount decisions made verbally without a written approval trail. No communication to CS about non-standard pricing.
**Sales process change**: All discounts >20% require a written approval from Finance (Oliver) with a documented rationale. CS is CC'd on any non-standard pricing deal at close, so renewal conversations start with full context.
