## Template 1: Sales Outreach Email Sequence

### Cold Email (Email 1 of 3)

```
Subject: [specific reference to their company or a recent signal — not "quick question"]

[First name],

[1-2 sentences: specific reason you're reaching out to them specifically. Reference
something real — a job posting, a funding announcement, a product launch, a piece
of content they published. This must not be generic.]

[1 sentence: the pain you've identified that matches what your product solves.
Frame in their terms, not your product terms.]

[1 sentence: one-line proof — "We helped [similar company in same industry] [specific
outcome in their terms, e.g., 'reduce their incident response time by 70%']."]

Worth a 15-minute call this week to see if there's a fit?

[First name + Last name]
[Title] | [Company]
```

---

### Follow-Up Email (Email 2 of 3 — send Day 5 if no reply)

```
Subject: Re: [same thread]

[First name],

Following up in case [first email] got buried.

One thing I forgot to mention: [new piece of information — a relevant case study,
a specific feature you did not mention, a recent company news item that makes the
timing more relevant]. 

The specific reason I thought of you: [one sentence connecting the new information
to their situation].

Still worth 15 minutes?

[First name]
```

---

### Breakup Email (Email 3 of 3 — send Day 12 if no reply)

```
Subject: Closing the loop

[First name],

I've reached out a couple of times and haven't heard back — totally understand
if the timing is wrong or this isn't relevant.

I'll stop reaching out after this. But if [pain point they likely have] becomes
a priority in the next quarter, [your product/company] helps [ICP companies like
theirs] [specific outcome].

If the timing is better in Q[X], I hope you'll keep us in mind.

[First name]

P.S. If you have 2 minutes to tell me why this missed the mark, I'd genuinely
appreciate the feedback. Reply with "not relevant because ___" if you have a moment.
```

---

### Warm Outreach (Introduction / Inbound Lead Follow-Up)

```
Subject: [Specific to their context — e.g., "Following up on your trial" or
"[Mutual connection] suggested we connect"]

[First name],

[1 sentence establishing the warm context — who introduced you, or what they did
that triggered this reach-out.]

[1-2 sentences: you've done your homework. Reference something specific about
their company or role that is relevant to what your product does.]

I'd love to understand [specific thing about their situation — a challenge, a
goal, a context] to see if we might be a fit.

Do you have 20 minutes [specific day and time, or "this week or next"]?

[First name]
[Title | Company | Phone]
```
